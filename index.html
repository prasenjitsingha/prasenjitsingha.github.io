Key compliance milestones for a newly formed CSR Committee?

Formulate a formal CSR Policy identifying Schedule VII activities, establish an Annual Action Plan with budgets, and verify all implementing partners possess valid MCA CSR-1, 12A, and 80G registrations.

Calculating optimal sample size for a rural baseline survey?

Apply Cochran's formula: n = (Z² * p * q) / e², adjusted for cluster design effect (1.5 - 2.0). A representative sample of 450 to 600 households across 30 villages yields 95% confidence with 4% margin of error.

Optimal doctor-to-patient ratio for a rural Mobile Medical Unit?

Target 60 to 80 consultations per operating camp (4-5 clinical hours). Exceeding 100 consultations severely degrades diagnostic thoroughness and patient counseling.
